People before bookings

Safety is part of the product.

Any real launch needs professional legal review, identity and age verification, privacy safeguards, trained support and a clear response to coercion or harassment.

🔐 Private by defaultNever publish a home address or expose live location to strangers.
🪪 Verify adultsReal deployment needs a secure, privacy-conscious verification provider.
🤝 Ongoing consentAnyone can decline a request or stop a meeting at any time.
🚨 Report & supportBuild a real human support and escalation process before launch.
